Spin & Solve
Family game night · 2–6 players

Spin it. Guess it. Solve it.

Pass the device around, spin the wheel, and race to crack the phrase. Puzzles come from science, math, Massachusetts history and word play.

    • Spin, then name a consonant. Each one in the puzzle earns the wedge value.
    • Vowels cost 200 from your round score. Wrong letter? The wheel passes on.
    • Bankrupt wipes your round score. Lose a turn does what it says.
    • Solve any time on your turn. Win the round to bank your score plus a 250 bonus.
