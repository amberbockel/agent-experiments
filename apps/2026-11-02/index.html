Full bloom

Plant fraction seeds in the pot. Get as close to one whole as you dare. Go over, and everything wilts.

  • On your turn, draw a seed as many times as you like.
  • Bank your pot whenever you want. You score its percent: ¾ full is 75 points.
  • Hit exactly one whole for 100 points plus a 50 point bloom bonus.
  • Go over one whole and the round scores nothing.
