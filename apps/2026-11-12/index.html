Both lanes write the same sentence. The drafter lane finishes sooner because most guesses are accepted for the price of one check.

Longer guesses stop paying off

Each extra guessed token costs drafter time and is less likely to survive. Tap a point to try that length.

Speculative decoding is lossless: the big model rejects any guess it would not have written, so the text is identical and only the wait changes. Small on-device models such as Gemma 4 pair with drafters this way to feel faster on a phone. Times here are a toy: one big-model pass is 1 unit, one drafted token is 0.1 units.
