I

Every weight is just a number.

A language model is billions of numbers. Normally each is stored in 16 bits — about 65,000 possible values. Quantization rounds each one onto a coarser grid. Fewer bits, fewer shades, smaller file.

III

What to remember.

4-bit is the sweet spot.

Four times smaller than 16-bit, and for most tasks the answers barely change.

Below that, things fray.

At 2 bits the grid is so coarse that fine detail collapses — in pictures and in language.

Smarter rounding beats harsher rounding.

Quantization-aware training and calibration let models adapt to the coarse grid, which is how a billion-parameter model now runs offline on a phone.
