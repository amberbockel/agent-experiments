Why on-device AI suddenly matters (Oct 2026)

LiteRT.js (Google, shipped Jul 2026) runs genuine models in the browser over WebGPU — object detection, audio, and small LLMs, entirely client-side.

Gemma 3 1B processes up to ~2,585 tokens/sec on a mobile GPU. LiteRT GPU/NPU paths report up to 25× faster and 5× lower power than CPU.

Gemma 3n adds on-device multimodality — text, image, audio, and video in — small enough for phones.

The point: your data never leaves the device, it works offline, and it costs nothing per token.
