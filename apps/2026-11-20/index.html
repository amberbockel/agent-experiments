Does it fit?

Sizes and speeds are illustrative. A phone gives a model about half its RAM; add 0.6 GB for the context cache; speed assumes 60 GB/s of memory bandwidth. The signal figure is measured live on the sample above. Rule of thumb: 25 dB and up is hard to tell from the original, under 12 dB is broken.
