The ink never lies.

Every case lands on your desk as a scrap of evidence with a word to recover. You work it out, then write it in your own hand and photograph it for the lab.

If your letters are clean, the clue decodes and the trail moves on. If they smudge, the lab tells you which letters to fix. Neat writing cracks the case.
