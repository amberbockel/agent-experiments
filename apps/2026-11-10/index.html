Blurryclick

You are the computer-use agent. Decide how sharp a screenshot to buy, then click.

Agents that drive apps look at a screenshot and answer with an x,y position. Every pixel they look at costs tokens (roughly width times height divided by 750), so sharper vision is slower and pricier. Blurrier vision is cheap, but it moves the click to the nearest pixel centre.

Six screens. Start at the cheapest view, pay to sharpen when you need to, and land every click for as few tokens as you can.

Built around the new wave of computer-use agents trending on Product Hunt, such as NeuralAgent 3.0 and Ara, which act on your screen by pixel coordinates.

How sharp should the screenshot be?

You pay once per sharpness you look at. Clicks snap to the centre of the nearest pixel you can see.
